Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the app, we also store the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the core functionality of Spendly — logging expenses, generating spending summaries, and maintaining your account. We do not sell your personal data or expense records to third parties.

Data Storage

Your information is stored in our database with reasonable technical safeguards in place to protect it from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. While we take security seriously, no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers for infrastructure such as hosting and email delivery. These providers only receive the minimum data necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page.
